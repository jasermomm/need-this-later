# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be useful, specific, and safe for people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be sent through the private security/contact channel listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, review spaces, and when someone is representing the project in public.
